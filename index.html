Agro-Export Technical Standards & Suta Guide

Mithila Makhana & Roasted Chana Sattu specifications for B2B export calls

Makhana (Fox Nuts / Gorgon Nuts) Suta Sizing Matrix

6+ Suta (Jumbo) Top Tier

Diameter: 19mm - 22mm+

Export prime grade. Preferred by buyers in USA, Canada, UK, and UAE for luxury retail pouches. Maximum puff volume & white luster.

5 - 6 Suta Standard Bulk

Diameter: 16mm - 19mm

Highest volume commercial export grade. Optimal balance of price-to-size for Indian merchant exporters and international private-label snack packers.

4 - 5 Suta Commercial

Diameter: 13mm - 16mm

Commercial / processing grade. Used for flavored snack lines, flour milling, catering packs, and sweet confectionery blends.

Crucial Lab Parameters:
• Moisture: < 8.5%
• Hard Seed (Kani): < 0.3%
• Broken / Chura: < 1.0%
• Bleach: 100% Unbleached

Roasted Chana Sattu (Bengal Gram Flour) Export Standards

Processing Method & Purity

Direct wood-fired sand roasted Bihar desi chana. Complete de-husking with mechanical air aspiration. Zero adulteration with yellow pea (matar) flour.

Nutritional & Packaging Benchmarks

Protein content 22g - 24g per 100g. Fine 80-100 mesh texture for instant drink solubility. Export packed in 500g nitrogen flushed vacuum pouches or 25kg multi-wall paper bags.
